# 2026-09-05 — Phase 3: vegetation, figures, and background

## What was built

- `src/photofield.js`: the photo's mean color per cell of a 48x44 grid over the frame, kept for the cherry's region (u 0.208 to 0.875, v 0.114 to 0.727), with a mask of the cells whose mean is blossom: pink to mauve (red above green, blue not below green) inside the cherry's box, the canopy's sun-lit top (warm white at u 0.50 to 0.72 between the hill ridge and v 0.23) and the lowest strands' lavender over the bluish street (u 0.38 to 0.52, v 0.62 to 0.69). `canopyColorAt(u, v)` gives a blossom its color (a lavender cell takes the nearest blossom cell's hue and saturation at its own lightness), `canopyMaskAt(u, v, margin)` bounds the silhouette. Baked by a session script from `japan.webp` through the compare tool's decoder; the mask is printed as ASCII when baked.
- `src/materials.js`: one factory (`makeMaterial`, `foliageMaterial`) for every material; `MATERIALS.lit` switches the scene from `MeshBasicMaterial` to `MeshStandardMaterial` with the normal and roughness maps the texture generators already produce. `surface()` in `instancing.js` goes through it.
- `src/vegetation.js`: the weeping cherry: trunk and seven main limbs as tapered tubes along Catmull-Rom splines (the limb ends are photo positions at chosen depths, so the canopy's shell projects onto the mask; each limb and sub-branch is cut where its projection leaves the mask, one step short, so no wood pokes out of the blossom), three sub-branches per limb, 760 hanging strands (the last 160 from the far-left limbs, the long ones) as thin tubes that arc out then fall, each ending at its last blossom, and about 30,700 blossom cards in one InstancedMesh: a canvas-drawn cluster of soft petal discs with a warm light rim (the back-lit rim tint) and faint stamens, alpha-tested, two-sided, each colored from the field at its photo position with hue, saturation and lightness jitter (a sample whose hue is not pink, the sun glare's, is pulled to pale pink at the same lightness), half facing the photo camera and half upright at random headings. Cards and stems stay out of the right machiya, its eave band and the fence's roofed wall in world space, and out of two photo boxes where the photo shows the trunk and the lamp post in front of the canopy. Four pines of layered needle-cluster cards, whorls from crown to ground, standing on the hillside beyond the bend; the planter shrub on the walkway behind the fence's jog, its top above the roof's ridge (shaded from the sampled green to its lit top), a small potted plant beside the big pot and the potted plant on the left wall as leaf cards; grass tufts at the wall bases; moss patches on the wall faces at the gutter level.
- `src/figures.js`: the person as legs, torso, arms, collar, head and hair, 1.28 m tall from the camera model, jacket in the sampled blue, trousers darker (by eye), no face.
- `src/background.js`: the three far roofs as gabled houses (a plaster gable band under the roof, a dark wall below it in the photo's color there, two kawara slopes) whose front slope covers the photo's roof box, standing clear of the far street's paving with their walls reaching their ground; ground strips beside the far street beyond the bend and a short hillside past the paving's end (`farGroundY` in `src/layout.js`), all outside and below the paving so none of it shows from the photo view; the hill as a surface leaning back with depth, textured with cellular tree crowns (a jittered point grid, each crown lit on its sun side) over the photo's row gradient (every row's mean is the sampled color at that photo row, exactly); the mountains as three planes, a pale far ridge whose tops sit at v 0.25 and a blue range below it, both sampled from the 48-grid field, and the dull-green near ridge.
- `tools/placement.js` (run by `npm test`, also `npm run placement`): casts the photo camera's ray through each entry of `PLACEMENT_CHECKS` in `src/layout.js` and requires the first mesh hit to be the named one. Nineteen landmarks. This retires the phase 2 lesson; its proof is in `docs/learning/gate-proofs.md`.

## What was believed and proved false

- **Pink means blossom.** The photo's canopy against the sun glare (u 0.50 to 0.72, v 0.13 to 0.23) is warm white, red above green but blue below it, so the pink test dropped it and the render showed the hill there; the lowest strands over the bluish street (u 0.38 to 0.52, v 0.62 to 0.69) read lavender with green above red, so they were dropped too. Both are blossom by position and got their own rules. Cell distance 0.0921 to 0.0853 across those two fixes and the pines.
- **The plan's evergreen box is where the pines are.** The box starts at v 0.22; the photo's cells at v 0.25 over u 0.29 to 0.37 are pale haze, and the crowns sit from v 0.26 down. Crowns placed at v 0.22 to 0.25 cost 0.5 in two cells.
- **The stone wall's far end can stay where phase 2 put it.** Phase 2 lengthened the ashlar wall to z = -5.3 for one light cell; that end hides the annex's near door, which the photo shows dark red-brown at u 0.08 to 0.14 (cells (0.104, 0.75) and (0.104, 0.795), sampled as `doorRed`). The wall ends at z = -4.8 again and the door stands at z -6.7.
- **Denser is always better for the cells.** Fewer, larger fill cards (12 to 20 per branch step instead of 18 to 30) cost 0.001 of cell distance for no measurable frame time; the density stays. A blossom card drawn as five-petalled flowers instead of overlapping discs had less opaque area and cost 0.0035: the discs stay, with three faint stamens instead of six dark ones that read as faces up close.
- **Hiding a mesh proves the placement gate.** `Raycaster` ignores `visible`, so a hidden trunk still passed; the proof removes the mesh instead (see the gate proof).
- **Perf readings are stable.** Consecutive `npm run perf` runs after the same build gave 5.9, 9.7, 2.7 and 2.6 ms medians; the last two, with nothing else running, are the numbers. The blossom cards' overdraw is the frame cost now (p95 about 6 ms at 1920x1080).
- **Photo positions at a chosen depth put the pines somewhere sensible.** They put three of them in the far street, trunks showing behind the lamp post in the photo view. Where the photo has them is the hillside behind the far houses; at 40 to 50 m their trunks hide behind the houses, the bend and the canopy, and the crowns keep their photo size at about 7 m tall.
- **A hillside can rise as far as it likes behind the scene.** One rising to y = +19 at z = -140 showed above the horizon and hid the mountains (SSIM 0.4160 to 0.3469 in one run). It stops 16 m past the paving.
- **A blossom in a lavender cell should be lavender.** Colored from those cells the strand ends were blue; colored from the nearest pink cell they were too dark for the photo's light cells there. The nearest pink cell's hue and saturation at the lavender cell's own lightness scored best (SSIM 0.3785 to 0.4017).

## What the critics found, and what changed

The independent critic reproduced the gates, the perf numbers and the placement proof, then found, in its order: the pines planted in the far street with a trunk visible behind the lamp post; a limb and its sub-branches rising above the canopy into the sky, where the mask also admitted sun-glare cells; the lavender rule tinting the strand ends blue; stems running past their blossoms into mid-air and through the fence roof and the machiya's eave, with cards inside the machiya's wall; tube winding inside-out; `material()` and the fascia bypassing the factory; the planter shrub through the fence roof, the small shrub under the walkway, the potted plant a 3.4 m hedge; the far houses floating 20 m above the ground; devlog counts from an earlier build; and nits (a check that accepted any cherry mesh, a cloud cell in the mask, `background.js` listed twice, moss "lying" while standing, no rim tint). Every item changed: the pines stand on the hillside beyond the bend on a ground strip, a far-street placement check catches trunks there; limbs and sub-branches are clipped to the mask one step short of its edge, the sun-lit top counts only below the hill ridge and v 0.17; the lavender cells keep the silhouette but take pink hue at their own lightness; stems end at their last blossom and stems and cards stay out of the machiya, its eave band and the fence roof; the tube winding faces outward; every material goes through `makeMaterial`; the shrub stands on the bed behind the wall with its top above the ridge, the small shrub sits in a small pot on the walkway, the potted plant is 1.8 m long; the far houses' bodies reach the ground strips; the counts below are measured from the built scene; the mask is bounded to the cherry's box; the rim tint is in the card texture (a warm light rim on each petal disc); the comments and AGENTS.md corrected. The canopy's mean over the mask cells was 0.048 darker per cell than the photo's before the fixes; the blossom colors now carry a 1.05 factor and the strand-end lightness, and the cell distance over the frame went from 0.0855 to 0.0854 with SSIM 0.4160 to 0.4017 (the pines' larger, nearer crowns and the stems beyond the mask had been matching the photo's texture for the wrong reasons).

A second critic round on that tree confirmed the mask clipping, the strand ends, the stems, the winding, the factory and the counts, and found that grounding things had introduced two visible defects and left one: the far houses' bodies, now reaching the ground, put a 13 m light plaster wall across the far street (two of the frame's worst cells); the new ground strips showed above the paving and beside the trunk at four places; and the planter shrub, moved behind the fence, hung with its base inside the roof because there is no bed at that x. Fixed: the strips start beyond the walkway and the landing (z < -24) and 3.4 m out from the street's centre line, the houses' walls stop 3.2 m short of the paving and carry the photo's dark color below a plaster gable band, and the shrub stands on the walkway behind the fence's jog, 2.9 m tall so its top clears the ridge as the photo shows. The critic also noted the pines were crowns on 15 m poles from a hillside view: their whorls now run all the way down to the ground.

## How it was checked

The photo's cell means over the cherry were printed as a table with a pinkness tag before the tree was built, and the 48-grid mask as ASCII after each bake. After each build: `npm test` (shot, compare, placement, thresholds), the worst cells ranked from `out/scores.json`, photo-versus-render crops of the cherry, the strands, the evergreens, the far roofs, the person and the shrubs, probes at the plan's landmarks, and the 21 orbit and close-up views (three orbit angles, five of my own, thirteen from the phase 2 critics) inspected at native size: the tree reads as a weeping cherry from below and from the side, the pines as layered conifers, the far roofs as houses, the person as a figure, the door red, the weeds along the stair edges. The placement gate was made to go red by removing the trunk mesh before it was trusted.

## Bounds worth knowing

- The blossoms' colors are the photo's colors at their photo positions: from any other angle the canopy still carries the photo's lighting (light top right, mauve core, lavender lowest strands) painted onto the 3D volume. That is the contract (the photo view scores) but phase 4's lighting will have to work with, not against, those baked tints; `MATERIALS.lit` switches the materials, not the colors.
- The cards are flat quads: up close a blossom is a soft blob, and a card seen edge-on is a line. Half face the photo camera, so from the side the canopy is thinner.
- The strands end where the photo's mask ends, so their lengths encode the photo, not botany; the world-space exclusions cover the machiya, its eave band and the fence roof, the two photo boxes the trunk and the lamp post, and nothing else.
- The hill's texture is one 1024 px sheet stretched over about 800 m of ridge; its crowns render about 4 px across in the photo view, coarser than the photo's grain, and from an orbit close to the hill they are 2 m cobbles.
- The far houses' bodies are plaster boxes with gable ends standing on the ground strips; their far sides and the bend's corner house are still block-out, and the hillside stops 16 m past the paving with the hill backdrop far behind it.
- The placement gate sees geometry, not pixels: a card whose alpha-tested texel is discarded still counts as a hit, and the far-street check passes as long as paving is the first hit at one point.
- The far houses' walls run 12 to 14 m from their roofs to their ground: at 26 to 32 m their roofs sit near eye level while the street there is 11 m below, so the wall below the gable is long. The photo shows dark there, and that is the color it carries.
- The canopy stops at x = 4.6 so it stays out of the machiya, which leaves the upper wall bare at u 0.75 to 0.83, v 0.27 to 0.32 (two of the frame's worst cells). Reaching over the roof would need the canopy to pass through the building.
- Nothing yet checks that a piece stands on something: the grounding lesson is queued in `docs/learning/lessons.md`.
- Frame time is now the blossom overdraw; a lit material in phase 4 doubles the fragment cost of every card.
